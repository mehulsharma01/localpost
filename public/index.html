Questions

What is "local market data"?

Search demand for cleaning in your area, seasonal peaks, your competitors' public reviews, your own reviews and local events. It decides what your posts say each month.

Do you post for me?

No, on purpose. You keep control of your accounts. Posting takes about 2 minutes a week.

Whose photos do you use?

Yours wherever possible. For lifestyle scenes we use licensed or custom photos, never presented as your staff or customers.

Who is the model in my posts?

A realistic human model made for your business. Your model's face is only used for you, never for another cleaning business, and appears in fresh scenes every month: at home, on moving day, hosting guests and more. You can pick one of 3 free models for your sample posts, choose from many male and female models on the plan, or use your own team photo instead. Models are shown as customers and everyday people, never as your staff.

Will my posts look the same as other cleaners'?

No. Each post is rebuilt from your own data: the searches in your town, your reviews word for word, your services, your colours and logo, and your chosen face. Two businesses in the same city still get different posts.

Will this get me more customers?

Nobody can honestly promise bookings. We promise a page that looks active, local and trusted, built on what your town is looking for.

# Image prompting (model photos for posts)

The user's locked prompt format for Nano Banana Pro (Higgsfield, 2k). Every image prompt is built on these 6 pillars, in this order. Never use buzzwords like "photorealistic", "ultra HD" or "hyperrealistic".

1. **Core Action & Authentic Subject:** describe the subject naturally. Always force: candid expression, authentic skin textures, fine pores, stray hairs, natural non-plastic look. Put the model's exact face description first (brows, eyes, nose, lips, jaw, skin, hair).
2. **Environment & Cinematic Layering:** name foreground, midground and background elements for depth (reflections on windows, dust particles in sunbeams, subtle shadows).
3. **The Anti-Stock Wardrobe:** detailed fabric textures (e.g. heavyweight cotton showing weave lines, natural creases, realistic fabric weight). Plain, unbranded clothes and shoes.
4. **Cinematic Camera Direction:** shot on a 35mm or 50mm prime lens, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the subject, softly blurred background (bokeh), dynamic candid perspective.
5. **High-Contrast Commercial Lighting & Color Logic:** crisp volumetric sunlight from the side, dramatic directional shadows, high midtone contrast, deep saturated colour (especially deep royal blues and punchy canary yellows), clean luminous skin tones with an under-glow.
6. **Negative Quality Enforcements (always appended):** "No flat clinical stock-photo lighting, no smooth airbrushed skin artifacts, no clean vector-like digital clothing shapes, zero artificial 3D look." Plus: no text, no logos, no brand marks.
